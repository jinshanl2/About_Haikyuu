KENMA KOZUME

About Kenma

Kenma Kozume (Japanese: 孤爪 研磨 Kozume Kenma) was previously a second-year student from Nekoma High. He was the Volleyball Team's setter and was referred to as the "heart" and "brain" of the team by his teammates.
