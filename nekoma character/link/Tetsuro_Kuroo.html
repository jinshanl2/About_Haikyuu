TETSURŌ KUROO



About Kuroo

Tetsurō Kuroo (Japanese: 黒尾 鉄朗 Kuroo Tetsurō) was a third-year student at Nekoma High. He was the captain and one of the starting middle blockers for the Boys' Volleyball Team.
