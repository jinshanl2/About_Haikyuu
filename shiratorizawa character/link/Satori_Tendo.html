SATORI TENDŌ

About Tendō

Satori Tendō (Japanese: 天童 覚 Tendō Satori) was a third-year student at Shiratorizawa Academy and one of the volleyball team's middle blockers. He was often referred to as the Guess Monster (Japanese: ゲスげすモンスターもんすたー Gesu Monsutā) by other teams.
