HAIKYUU



About Haikyuu

Haikyuu!! (ハイキュー!!, Haikyū!!, from the kanji 排球 "volleyball") is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Haruichi Furudate. It was serialized in Shueisha's shōnen manga magazine Weekly Shōnen Jump from February 2012 to July 2020, with its chapters collected in 45 tankōbon volumes. The story follows in the view of a boy named Shoyo Hinata, he is determined to become a great volleyball player despite his small stature. He was influenced in playing volleyball when one day on his way home he saw "Little Giant" from the Tv, he was influenced by the shine of the volleyball player and wishes to be like him.

An anime television series adaptation produced by Production I.G, aired on MBS from April to September 2014, with 25 1episodes. A second season aired from October 2015 to March 2016, with 25 episodes. A third season aired from October to December 2016, with 10 episodes. A fourth season was released in two split cours from January to December 2020, with 25 episodes. Also, there is four OVA through out the seasons show in us parts of their life that wasn't included in the anime season. Two anime film series will be released in two parts, which serves as the finale of the series. The first one is titled Haikyuu!! The Dumpster Battle.

Plot

In this anime, it startes from the point of view of a boy named Hinata Shoyo. It starts when Hinata saw "Little Giant" and was influened by him to play volleyball Thus, he strike to join in Karasuno High school where "Little Giant" was in. In there he met players that have the same goals as him to stay on the volleyball court longer to play, to sweat, to experience the excitement.They hace many other teams that have the dreams. Everyone in the anime have their own life and experience but they all try to achieve the same goal. This anime starts from the view of Hinata and show the life of everyone else.
